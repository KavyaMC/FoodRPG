Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Food RPG handles user data within the ManasChakshu ecosystem.

2. Data Collected

When using account-based features, we may collect:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password (securely stored in encrypted form)
  • Game progress and save data
  • Achievements and unlockables
  • Settings and preferences

3. How Data Is Used

Collected data is used to:

  • Enable account login and authentication
  • Save and restore game progress
  • Track achievements and progression
  • Support accessibility and gameplay features
  • Improve system stability and experience

We do not sell user data.

4. Data Storage

Data may be stored:

  • Locally on the user’s device (offline saves)
  • On secure servers (for account-based features and cloud saves)

5. Third-Party Services

Future integrations (such as authentication, hosting, or payments) may use third-party providers. These services may have their own privacy policies.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user data, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Future Features

Food RPG may introduce:

  • Expanded account features
  • Multiplayer systems
  • In-app purchases
  • Store systems

These will be governed by updated privacy terms when released.

8. Changes to Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time. Updates will be communicated via official announcements.
